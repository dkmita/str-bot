### Local tips

**Q:** Where should we eat?
A: The Hudson is a walkable mile away (walk-ins OK), and Mesa Grill has runway views and happy hour 3:30–5:30. Book Elote and Mariposa ASAP; also try Vespa or Gerardo's (Italian), Indian Gardens (breakfast/lunch), and Hilltop Deli (lunch). Full list: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1oH1EnmbFNWFDdJeIsmjfelEmr9isU1mJzaSoITuMQ0Q/edit?usp=sharing

**Q:** What spas or saunas do you recommend?
A: Options include L'Apothecary at L'Auberge (luxury), Sedona New Day Spa (local), and The Spa at Outbound Sedona (short walk). Mii amo's sauna requires a booked service, and there are also Los Abrigados (dry sauna) and a new infrared sauna nearby.

**Q:** Where can we hike or bike?
A: Bike-friendly, uncrowded trails start a 5-minute walk away and lead to Broken Arrow and the Chapel; try Peccary → Mystic → Hogwash → Pigtail → Chapel. For mountain-bike tips, ask Thunder Mountain Bikes.

**Q:** Is there creek access?
A: Yes, about 10 minutes' drive south, and Buddha's Beach is the most popular spot. Oak Creek Canyon is closed (as of Jul 2026).

**Q:** What day trips do you recommend?
A: Montezuma Castle (~30 min) and Jerome (~50 min). Flagstaff (~1 hr) has Sunset Crater, Wupatki, and Lowell Observatory.

**Q:** Can you get us into golf at Seven Canyons?
A: No. It's a private, members-only club.

**Q:** Where is the house in relation to the rest of Sedona?
A: Between Tlaquepaque and the Chapel. It's more scenic than Uptown and minutes from everything, with a trailhead shuttle nearby.
