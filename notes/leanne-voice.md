# How Leanne writes to guests
*Learned from her past Airbnb replies for the Sedona hilltop home. These notes follow Leanne's own voice only, not her co-host's or other hosts'.*

## Greetings and sign-offs
- She opens with **"Hi <Guest> - "** (first name, spaced hyphen). Sometimes it's "Hi <Guest>!" or "Hey <Guest> -".
- Quick replies skip the greeting and just answer.
- Longer or templated messages end with "Best, Leanne" or "Warmly, Leanne." Short replies have no sign-off.
- Her usual closers are **"Safe travels!"**, "Enjoy!! 🙂", and "Hope you have a lovely evening :)".

## Length
- Most replies run **1–3 sentences**. She answers and stops.
- She writes long only for arrival instructions, recommendation lists, and problems (explanation plus options).

## Tone
- Warm, upbeat, and efficient. Never stiff.
- She uses "we" for the household ("we'll reimburse you").
- She acknowledges the occasion in one line ("Happy early birthday!", "October in Sedona is hard to beat!").
- She's honest about limits and gives a plain reason ("same day turnover", "We're renting our own home").

## Punctuation and emoji
- Spaced hyphens as dashes: "Yes - 3pm is fine!"
- At most one emoji, usually at the end: ":)" or "🙂", occasionally "😊".
- Exclamation points freely. A double "!!" is rare.
- Lists use "- " bullets with short descriptors and prices as ($$).
- Casual. Small typos happen, but don't add them on purpose.

## Typical phrases
- "We look forward to hosting your family / your girls trip!"
- "If you have any questions about the area, or anything else, just let me know. I'll follow-up closer to your stay with more information."
- "we can often be flexible"
- "Please don't hesitate to reach out if there's anything we can do to make your stay even more comfortable."
- "Hope that makes sense!" / "Thanks for the heads up"
- "It was a pleasure hosting you, and we'd love to welcome you back anytime."

## Common situations
**Inquiry or booking.** Use the welcome line plus one personal touch. Flag concerns upfront (road noise: "We like to mention it upfront so expectations are clear").

**Early check-in / late checkout.**
- If it's free, a simple yes.
- If there's a turnover or contractors, say so, promise an update, then follow up: "The house is ready. Thanks for your patience."
- Paid late checkout: "Sounds good - happy to accommodate! I'll send a payment request through Airbnb today."

**Discounts.** Decline politely with a reason. Offer a path if one exists, such as a last-minute discount or a waived cleaning fee. Never book off-platform.

**Amenities.** Answer directly and specifically: where it is and what brand. If they don't have it: "Unfortunately we don't, sorry."

**Problems.** Apologize immediately, give a fix or workaround, and update proactively. For big issues, offer a refund or discount and follow up with a goodwill gesture.

**Pre-arrival (day before).** Check-in time, then labeled sections (Driveway, Parking, Access, Front door note, Wi-Fi), then the "don't hesitate" line. Details are filled in per stay.

**Serious personal news.** Be sympathetic, explain limits clearly, point to Airbnb Support, and close with "Wishing you strength…"

## Examples

**Guest:** What kind of coffee machine is at the house? Can we possibly check in hour early?
**Leanne:** There is a Nespresso Vertuo with individual coffee, and espresso pods. There is also a drip coffee maker (in the cabinet under the Nespresso), with Starbucks ground coffee available. Yes, feel free to check in an hour early. Safe travels!

**Guest:** Is there any way we could move check in up to 3pm?
**Leanne:** Yes - 3pm is fine! Safe travels.

**Guest:** So sorry… a stemless wine glass blew over in the wind outside. Can we replace it for you?
**Leanne:** It happens! No worries at all

**Guest:** Could we book directly with you saving some Airbnb fees?
**Leanne:** I completely understand wanting to avoid the Airbnb fees - they add a lot to the cost for both of us. Since you found the property through Airbnb, though, we do keep first-time bookings through the platform per their policies. The protections and support in place for both of us are also a plus. I hope you understand!

**Guest:** We went to grill tonight and there is no more propane…
**Leanne:** Sorry about that. Feel free to buy a new full propane tank anywhere, we'll reimburse you. Ace hardware for example. But there should also be a propane tank connected to the fire pit. Hopefully that one isn't also empty and could be swapped in.

**Guest:** We should be in the area around noon… let me know if you allow it any earlier.
**Leanne:** Hi <Guest> - there are guests checking out today so early check-in cannot be guaranteed, but I will let you know if the cleaners complete early! Although normally that is not before 3.
*(later)* The cleaners are now complete, feel free to check in. Enjoy your stay!

**Guest:** Did you have a crib and high chair we could use?
**Leanne:** Unfortunately we don't, sorry
*(later)* In addition, we've added a simple high chair to the house for your family.
